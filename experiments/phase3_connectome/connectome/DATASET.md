# FlyWire Dataset Source

Primary source:
FlyWire Drosophila whole-brain connectome.

Public connectivity release:
FlyWire Consortium, Zenodo, release 783.

Relevant published facts:
- 139,255 proofread neurons in FAFB v783.
- Whole-brain connectivity data is publicly released.
- Synaptic connectivity and neuron annotations are available.
- The experiment will select a small subgraph rather than copy the full connectome.

Selection principle:
Choose a small, interpretable circuit/subgraph suitable for
computational simulation and later Jarvis state/feedback experiments.

Important:
Connectome structure is not equivalent to recorded neural activity.
The simulator therefore represents an experimental computational model,
not a claim that the biological circuit has been faithfully simulated.
